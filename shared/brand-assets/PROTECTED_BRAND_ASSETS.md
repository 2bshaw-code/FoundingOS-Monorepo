# Protected Brand Assets

`foundit-mark.svg` and `foundmeat-mark.svg` are protected master assets, with `foundit-mark.svg` serving the FoundThis brand mark for compatibility.
`foundtalent-mark.svg` is a protected master asset.

- Do not regenerate, redraw, simplify, recolor, theme, or convert them to monochrome.
- Do not replace them with initials, generated pictograms, dynamic logos, or tenant branding.
- FoundThis remains `#FFD600`; FoundMeat remains `#B00020`.
- FoundTalent remains `#F97316`.
- Product and Founder surfaces must render these files through `FoundThisBrandMark` and `FoundMeatBrandMark`.
- Product and Founder surfaces must render the FoundTalent file through `FoundTalentBrandMark`.
- Brand resets and color-setting changes must not modify this package.

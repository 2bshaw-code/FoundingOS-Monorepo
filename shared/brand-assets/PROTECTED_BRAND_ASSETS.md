# Protected Brand Assets

`core_intelligence-mark.svg` and `foundmeat-mark.svg` are protected master assets, with `core_intelligence-mark.svg` serving the Intelligence brand mark for compatibility.
`core_workforce-mark.svg` is a protected master asset.

- Do not regenerate, redraw, simplify, recolor, theme, or convert them to monochrome.
- Do not replace them with initials, generated pictograms, dynamic logos, or tenant branding.
- Intelligence remains `#FFD600`; CoreOperations remains `#B00020`.
- CoreWorkforce remains `#F97316`.
- Product and Founder surfaces must render these files through `IntelligenceBrandMark` and `CoreOperationsBrandMark`.
- Product and Founder surfaces must render the CoreWorkforce file through `CoreWorkforceBrandMark`.
- Brand resets and color-setting changes must not modify this package.

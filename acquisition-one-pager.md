# FoundingOS Acquisition One-Pager

**FoundingOS is the operating system for founder-run businesses**: one core
platform, three suites, and one system of record for customers, people, and
decisions.

## Platform

- **Core.Operations** — customers, orders, inventory, billing, delivery, and
  messaging workflows.
- **Core.Workforce** — applicants, recruiters, jobs, and workforce
  intelligence.
- **Core.Intelligence** — first-party KPIs, funnels, reports, and decision
  support.

## Backbone

One tenant-scoped identity model, one console shell, one messaging
abstraction, one external mapping service, one orchestration event model,
and one unified telemetry store. See [shared-schema.md](./shared-schema.md).

## Commercial model

Buyers license suites independently or bundle them. Messaging-platform buyers
typically activate OPS + INT; fintech buyers activate OPS; Jio-style
full-platform buyers activate OPS + WORK + INT; roll-ups can start with INT.

## Risk reduction

Legacy vertical products have been removed and excluded from the buyer-facing
architecture, reducing regulatory and operational complexity.

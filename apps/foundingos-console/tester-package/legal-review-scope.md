# Legal Review Scope

**Version: v2026-08-30.1**

> DRAFT — this document defines the scope of the Commercial Lawyer
> reviewer's (`LAW-REVIEW`) engagement.

## In scope

The Legal Reviewer's engagement covers review of:

- The user interface presented to Testers and Investors.
- These agreements (Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, NDA, and the
  role-specific agreements).
- Compliance posture (see `compliance-notes.md`).
- Legal exposure of the System as presented in this evaluation program.

## Explicitly out of scope

The Legal Reviewer is **not** reviewing, and is **not responsible for**:

- Business viability of FoundRetail or the wider FoundingOS ecosystem.
- Financial projections or forecasts.
- Module business logic or technical correctness.
- Investor decisions made using this System.

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, FoundRetail excludes
liability for:

- Bugs.
- Outages.
- Data loss.
- Incorrect metrics.
- Misinterpretation of dashboards.
- Tester misuse.
- Investor assumptions arising from use of this pre-release System.

## What this does not exclude

Nothing in this document excludes or limits liability that cannot lawfully
be excluded or limited — for example, liability for death or personal
injury caused by negligence, or for fraud.

# FoundingOS Investor Materials

## Positioning
FoundingOS is the operating console brand for the multi-brand workspace ecosystem. FoundThis covers internal services and supporting operations.

## What the platform includes
- Brand-specific consoles
- Shared UI shell and routing
- CRM and module surfaces
- Launch and deployment documentation

## Why it matters
The rename clarifies the public brand while preserving implementation stability in the shared package layer.

# The OS Suite — for Gupshup

Gupshup's whole thesis is conversational commerce — helping businesses sell
and support customers over WhatsApp. But talk to any of the hundreds of
thousands of businesses running on Gupshup and the conversation is the easy
part; the backend behind it (stock tracking, delivery, invoicing,
reconciliation) is still spreadsheets and manual work. The OS Suite is the
natural next layer: it takes the conversation Gupshup already enables and
turns it into a running business — inventory, logistics, finance, and
workforce, all wired to the same WhatsApp thread.

Why this fits Gupshup rather than a generic ops tool: it doesn't compete
with Gupshup's messaging business, it extends it. A merchant who runs
fulfilment-to-cash through Gupshup's channel is far harder to churn than
one only using it for customer replies, and it opens a second revenue line
(platform fee + automation credits) on top of existing messaging volume.
UPI reconciliation is built in natively, which matters given how much of
Gupshup's base is India-first.

What ships:
- WhatsApp-first order intake and delivery updates.
- UPI and mobile money reconciliation unified into one ledger.
- Fulfilment-to-Cash running natively over the same conversations Gupshup
  already powers.

Ask: strategic acquisition, roughly $20–40M, to become Gupshup's
commerce-and-operations layer for emerging-market merchants.

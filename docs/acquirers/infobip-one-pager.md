# The OS Suite — for Infobip

Infobip owns the pipe — WhatsApp Business API, SMS, voice — that businesses
across Africa, India, and Southeast Asia run their customer conversations
through. What Infobip doesn't own is what happens after the message: the
inventory update, the delivery, the invoice, the reconciled payment. Right
now that's all still duct-taped together outside Infobip's platform. The OS
Suite is that missing layer, built to sit directly on top of the channel
Infobip already provides.

Why this matters for Infobip specifically: a customer running full
fulfilment-to-cash workflows through Infobip's messaging infrastructure is
a very different (and much stickier) customer than one just sending
notifications. This turns Infobip from a messaging vendor its customers
could theoretically swap out, into the platform their entire order-to-cash
loop runs through.

What ships:
- WhatsApp as the actual interface for order intake and delivery updates —
  not a channel Infobip's customers bolt commerce onto themselves.
- Mobile money reconciliation (M-Pesa, MTN MoMo, UPI) unified with the
  payment record, so the messaging layer and the money finally sit
  together.
- Fulfilment-to-Cash as the flagship loop, running end to end over
  Infobip's own messaging rails.

Ask: strategic acquisition, roughly $20–40M, to become Infobip's vertical
commerce and operations layer for emerging-market customers.

# The OS Suite — for Paystack

Paystack won on trust and simplicity for Nigerian and pan-African
merchants. The next fight isn't payments — it's who owns the merchant
relationship once payments become table stakes. Right now, a Paystack
merchant still runs order intake over WhatsApp by hand, tracks stock in a
spreadsheet, and reconciles deliveries separately from the payment record.
The OS Suite closes that loop and turns Paystack from "the checkout" into
"the system of record" for the merchant's whole business.

What it adds concretely:
- Order intake and delivery updates run through WhatsApp as the primary
  interface, not an SMS afterthought.
- Inventory, logistics, and invoicing are unified with the payment record
  Paystack already holds — one ledger instead of three disconnected ones.
- Mobile money and card reconciliation sit in the same place, so a merchant
  never has to stitch together "did this order get paid" across tools.

This is a retention play as much as a product one. A merchant running
their whole fulfilment-to-cash loop on top of Paystack is far stickier than
one who only touches Paystack at checkout — and far harder for a
competitor like Flutterwave to pry loose.

Ask: strategic acquisition (~$20–40M) to become Paystack's merchant
operations layer across Nigeria and pan-African markets.

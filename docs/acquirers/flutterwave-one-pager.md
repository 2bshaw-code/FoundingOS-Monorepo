# The OS Suite — for Flutterwave

Flutterwave moves the money. It doesn't run the business on either side of
the transaction. That's the gap: a merchant in Lagos or Nairobi accepting
Flutterwave payments is still tracking inventory in a notebook, taking
orders over WhatsApp by hand, and reconciling deliveries in a separate
spreadsheet. The OS Suite is that missing layer — retail, logistics, and
finance operations, wired directly into the payment rail Flutterwave
already owns.

Put simply: every payment Flutterwave processes is one end of a business
workflow. Right now Flutterwave only sees the payment. The OS Suite gives
it the whole order — inventory drop, delivery, invoice, reconciliation —
which means richer merchant data, stronger lock-in, and a natural upsell
from "payments provider" to "the system merchants run their business on."

**Product fit**
- WhatsApp is the order-intake and delivery-update channel — not a
  notification bolt-on.
- Payments and reconciliation are already built mobile-money-first (M-Pesa,
  MTN MoMo, plus the Flutterwave rails themselves).
- Fulfilment-to-Cash workflow (order → delivery → invoice → reconciled
  payment) is the flagship loop — it's the exact transaction lifecycle
  Flutterwave sits inside of today, just without the operations wrapped
  around it.

**Why this is worth Flutterwave owning rather than watching a competitor
build it**: merchant retention in fragmented markets is won by whoever
owns the operational workflow, not just the payment moment. Whoever wraps
the order, delivery, and reconciliation loop around a payment becomes very
hard to displace.

Target: strategic acquisition, roughly $20–40M, to fold in as Flutterwave's
merchant operations layer for Africa.

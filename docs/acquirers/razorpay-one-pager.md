# The OS Suite — for Razorpay

Razorpay has spent years pushing beyond payments into neobanking, payroll,
and lending (RazorpayX, Payroll). The OS Suite is the missing piece of that
expansion: the actual operations software — retail, logistics, and finance
workflows — that small and mid-market Indian businesses need day to day,
built around UPI and WhatsApp instead of retrofitted onto them.

Where it fits:
- RazorpayX already wants to be the financial backbone for SMBs. The OS
  Suite gives it the operational front end — order intake over WhatsApp,
  inventory and delivery tracking, and invoicing — that naturally feeds
  RazorpayX's ledger and lending data.
- UPI reconciliation is native, not bolted on, which matters for a
  merchant base that already expects UPI to just work everywhere.
- The Fulfilment-to-Cash workflow (order → delivery → invoice → UPI
  reconciliation) is the exact transaction chain Razorpay's payments and
  RazorpayX products sit at the edges of today — the OS Suite fills in the
  middle.

The pitch to Razorpay specifically: this isn't a new payments product to
integrate, it's the operations layer that makes RazorpayX's financial data
richer and makes Razorpay merchants stickier, because they're now running
their whole business — not just accepting payments — on Razorpay's stack.

Ask: strategic acquisition (~$20–40M) to extend Razorpay from payments and
neobanking into full merchant operations software for India's SMB market.

# FoundingOS — Investor Demo Script

A 10-minute walkthrough of what FoundingOS does today. Show the live product; do not
quote traction figures unless they come from SuperDash or Stripe on the day.

## 1. Positioning (quote verbatim — see [positioning.md](./positioning.md))

"FoundingOS is the operating system for founder-run businesses — one core platform,
three suites (Core.Operations, Core.Workforce, Core.Intelligence), that replaces a stack
of disconnected tools with a single system of record for customers, people, and
decisions."

## 2. The problem

Owner-operators pay for and switch between separate tools for selling, stock,
invoicing, marketing, hiring and staff. Data is typed twice and nobody sees the whole
business.

## 3. Homepage and pricing (www.foundingos.com)

Suites, workspaces and modular pricing: free Lite, Core from £19 per base workspace
(Retail & Logistics, Talent, HR or Health),
bolt-ons (+£25 Commerce Pro, +£35 Core.Intelligence), £89 Complete, Enterprise on
request. Source of truth: [pricing.md](./pricing.md).

## 4. Core.Operations — Retail & Logistics workspace

Pipeline and deals, customers, quotes, orders, POS, returns, inventory with shared
product photos, deliveries and drivers. Show a quote becoming an invoice in Commerce Pro
and the aged-debt view.

## 5. Core.Workforce — Talent and HR

Talent: jobs, candidates, interviews, offers and placements. HR: onboarding checklists,
rotas, timesheets, holiday and right-to-work. Health (Core.Operations): patients,
appointments, referrals and compliance for clinics and care providers.

## 6. FoundAI, Autopilot and WhatsApp

Ask FoundAI a question about the business. Show an Autopilot proposal: propose →
approve → execute, with the audit trail and reversal. FoundingOS never moves money
autonomously. Show FoundAI on WhatsApp.

## 7. Mobile app

The iOS/Android app reads and writes the same tenant data as the web app. Show the
Today screen (cards can be rearranged) and a Retail module with product images.

## 8. SuperDash (founder only)

Subscriptions, estimated MRR/ARR, upgrade requests, platform health, finance ledger,
marketing posts and private customer ratings. Use the demo-data switch if live numbers
are not ready to share — it is clearly labelled as illustrative.

## 9. Trust

Tenant-isolated data, role-based access, encrypted integration credentials, signed
Stripe webhooks that switch workspaces on and off (with out-of-order protection),
append-only audit trail, data export, and a published privacy and cookie notice. No
third-party scraping and no advertising trackers.

## 10. Close — evidence for a valuation

"The product is built. Value now depends on traction." Share, from SuperDash and Stripe:
paying customers, MRR/ARR, retention and churn, weekly active companies, Lite-to-paid
upgrade rate, customer ratings, acquisition cost, runway and the 12-month plan.

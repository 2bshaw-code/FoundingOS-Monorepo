"Each brand has its own independent front-end, its own survey pipeline, and its own engagement flow. When a user interacts with any brand, FoundingOS captures it instantly."

"Let me show you how the OS breathes. I'll submit a real survey — this will update engagement, category breakdown, anomaly score, and Autonomous intelligence in real time."

## 8. Anomalies
"Every spike, dip, or unusual pattern is logged. This is how the OS learns."
